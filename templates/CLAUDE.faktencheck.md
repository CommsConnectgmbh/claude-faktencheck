## Facts and evidence

- If you cannot support something with evidence, say "I don't know" or "unsupported". Do not guess.
- Base claims about code, project state, and configuration only on files you read in this session. Check libraries, APIs, and package names in documentation or registries, rather than relying on memory.
- Claim "it works", "tests pass", or "it's live" only when supported by a command run in this session.
- Calculate numbers, totals, and counts with scripts, never in your head.
- When working with documents, quote the relevant passages verbatim before analyzing them.
- Have someone other than the author review important results (the `faktenpruefer` agent or a second model).
